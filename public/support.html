Need help or found a bug? Open an issue at github.com/slamduncaan747/PokerAnalysis/issues. Include the poker site and, if a file won't import, a sample hand with player names removed.

Importing hands

Export hand histories from your poker client as .txt files (or a .zip of them) and open them in Import. Supported: PokerStars, GGPoker/Natural8, CoinPoker, ACR/Winning Poker Network, Winamax, Ignition/Bovada and most PokerStars-style formats. No-Limit Hold'em only; duplicates are skipped automatically.

Getting hands from your computer to your iPhone

  • Pairing code: open Sync & devices on both devices. Tap Show a code on one and enter it on the other. Hands and training progress sync both ways, directly between the devices.
  • Backup file: Sync & devices → Export backup, AirDrop the .zip to your iPhone, then Import a backup in the app.

How decisions are graded

Preflop decisions are compared with solver-style range charts. Postflop decisions are scored by estimating the expected value of every option against your opponent's likely range. For a deeper look at any heads-up flop, turn or river spot, tap Solve this spot to run a full GTO solver on your phone.
